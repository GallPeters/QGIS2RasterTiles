about=Packages the visible layers into one GeoPackage, copies the project and points it to the package (filters and unused fields removed), then runs the built-in "Generate XYZ tiles (GeoPackage)" at normal priority: once, or as several parallel workers that skip empty areas (verified: no overlap, no gap). Also merges tile GeoPackages (e.g. zooms 0-18 and 19). Requires QGIS 4.3 or later.
